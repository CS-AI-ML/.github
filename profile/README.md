# CS AI and ML Consultancy

We build production software with AI engineered into the parts where it is useful.

**[KonteksWerke](https://kontekswerke.co.za/)** is the public trading brand of CS AI and ML Consultancy (Pty) Ltd. This is the company's engineering organisation, founded by [Christo W. Strydom](https://github.com/ChristoGH) in Johannesburg, South Africa.

Our work covers document and evidence systems, knowledge graphs, retrieval, operational applications and the infrastructure that supports them.
